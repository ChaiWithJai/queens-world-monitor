03 / Creative brief

The assignment

Make a Queens print worth keeping after the crowd leaves.

Problem

MBIQ has borough legitimacy and an audience, but wall art is unproven. The launch must test demand without risking speculative inventory, muddy rights, or a generic tennis costume.

Audience

Queens natives and diaspora first; culturally curious visitors and accessible-print collectors second. They buy identity and story, not a souvenir.

Product

One numbered owned hero, three time-limited graphic editions, and two licensed artist editions. Two sheet sizes. One paper family.

Success

Six coherent works, signed rights before preorder, proofs approved, one post-close print batch, and a reusable playbook for Volume 02.

Creative guardrails

  1. Queens is the subject.Tennis is the moment, not the borrowed brand.
  2. Wall first.It can crop well for social, but it must live at 18 × 24.
  3. Real codes.Use place, route, language, light, and infrastructure with specificity.
  4. Rights before hype.No work enters the lineup without ownership or signed terms.
  5. Quality is the story.Paper, proof, edition, and packaging are part of the creative.
